GGLOTY – Discover. Connect. Book.

Ggloty is a technology-driven beauty discovery and booking platform connecting customers with talented makeup artists through location-based discovery, visual portfolios and convenient bookings. It empowers artists to showcase their work, expand their reach and grow their professional presence.

GGLOTY ACADEMY – Learn. Create. Grow.

Ggloty Academy is a professional education and training initiative dedicated to developing talent in the makeup and beauty industry. Through structured learning, practical training and industry-focused skill development, it empowers aspiring and established artists to turn their passion into professional opportunities and build successful careers.
